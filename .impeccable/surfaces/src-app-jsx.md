# Splitlie product surfaces

Mode: Operate for authenticated product surfaces; Persuade for `/welcome`; focused Operate for authentication.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/overview-a-grid.png`

Audience and job: individuals and small groups who need to understand personal spending, budgets, recurring bills, shared expenses, and balances quickly. The primary action is adding or reviewing a transaction. All current functionality, routes, data states, permissions, and real copy remain intact. No navigation item, chart, notification center, account module, report, goal, category manager, or capability may be added only because it appears in the visual comp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Splitlie becomes a compact personal-finance workspace where state, action, and explanation coexist in one viewport. It refuses the discarded oversized dark hero, large decorative gaps, and inherited card proportions.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained white and cool-gray working surfaces, deep green-black ink, Splitlie emerald and pale mint state accents, fine neutral rules, 10–12px corners, tabular figures, compact grotesk typography, and small familiar controls.

STORY: The user identifies the active space and month, understands Safe to Spend and the three monthly totals, notices meaningful signals, then moves directly into real transactions, bills, budgets, members, settings, or settlement work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow left rail and compact top utility bar frame an asymmetrical twelve-column Overview. Safe to Spend is wider but not heroic; Spent, Income, and Net share its band. Money Signals spans the next band. Real recent activity and Upcoming Bills begin below. Add transaction remains the primary action.

FORM: Approved comp A, the high-density modular finance grid. It was the first of three user-reviewed compositions and is governed by seed `5462297a`. Landing and authentication inherit its precision and palette, but `/welcome` uses oversized brand typography rather than dashboard composition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved decisions: none. The approved comp is structural guidance; its invented search, notification, goal, report, account, category, and unsupported transaction details must not be implemented.
