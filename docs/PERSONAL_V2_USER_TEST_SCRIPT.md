# Personal Space V2 — Prototype Test Script

## Sample

Recruit 5–8 people who manage at least some personal spending. Include frequent and inconsistent trackers. Do not explain feature intent before each task.

## Session structure (30–40 minutes)

1. **Context (5 min)** — How do you currently remember or review spending? What usually makes you stop?
2. **Quick capture (5 min)** — “Record a lunch you bought yesterday for 18 dollars.” Observe actions, corrections, and whether details feel safely collapsed.
3. **Repeat purchase (3 min)** — “Add a recent purchase again for today.” Observe discoverability and trust in the prefill.
4. **Dashboard comprehension (5 min)** — “Without clicking, tell me how this month is going and where the money went.” Record reading order.
5. **Weekly recap (5 min)** — “What changed from last week, and what would you do next?” Do not teach the comparison language.
6. **Signals (5 min)** — Which signal is useful, confusing, obvious, or untrustworthy? What evidence should support it?
7. **Habit loop (3 min)** — Use Weekly Check-in and Monthly Reflection. Is either motivating, neutral, or burdensome?
8. **Debrief (5 min)** — What would make you return next week? What should be removed? What is missing?

## Capture per participant

- Task completion and time; number and type of corrections
- Misclassified merchant, category, date, or currency
- First dashboard element noticed and recap comprehension
- Signal relevance rating (1–5) with explanation
- Check-in/reflection reaction and direct quotes
- Final decision: Keep / Modify / Remove / Continue testing

## Guardrails

- Never present these prototypes as validated features.
- Do not test paid LLM parsing, bank linking, OCR, or new Shared Space flows in this round.
- Reset or identify seeded data between sessions.
- Avoid “Was this easy?”; ask participants to explain what happened.
